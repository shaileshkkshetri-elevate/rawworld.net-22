Two crises. One arena.

Farmland is vanishing, the population is surging, and temperatures are rising. RAW turns that problem into a competition the whole country can follow.

It is a new genre, Agritainment, that joins agriculture, entrepreneurship and climate action. Every episode is a test of skill, innovation and real-world impact.

Practical wisdom meets science.

Professional farmers and agriculture graduates are judged on their own terms, then paired into teams. Neither is enough alone.

One tree, one vote.

289,973

verified trees planted as votes in a single month. Over 60% were fruit-bearing, and one vote came in from Romania.

  • Supporters vote by planting a perennial tree in a contestant's name.
  • Every tree is verified with photo and video evidence.
  • The audience's voice becomes carbon storage, biodiversity and green jobs.

Television with consequences.

Scored tasks took contestants out of the studio and into schools, ministries and another country.

Season One champions.

21 episodes over two production years, crowned at a Grand Finale where soil from all 77 districts was applied as a tika to the winner.
